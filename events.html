Events Page Intro

Events in Macon County!

Events Page Summary

Macon County hosts a wide variety of events throughout the year ranging from seasonal festivals and outdoor concerts to farmer's markets, craft fairs, and community theater performances. Whether you're a resident or a visitor, our community offers plentiful of opportunities to connect, celebrate, and explore what makes our mountain towns so vibrant and welcome.

We look forward to seeing you at an event soon!

2025 Events So Far

From food truck festivals to mural painting days, Macon County's community events bring folks together in every season.

Spring Food Truck Festival

Residents and visitors sampled local flavors at this outdoor food truck festival, complete with live music, lawn games, and plenty of sunshine! Families spread out across the lawn to enjoy dinner, desserts, and time in the fresh mountain air.

Pride in the Mountains Celebration

Macon County's Pride event brought people of all ages together to celebrate and share support in the community. Colorful booths, live performance, and family-friendly activities created a welcoming space where everyone could show their pride.

Downtown Mural Painting Day

Volunteers, local artists, and students worked side by side to add new color to downtown Franklin. Throughout the day, neighbors stopped by to watch the murals come to life, share ideas, and cheer on the artists transforming blank walls into community stories.

Community Theater Shakespeare Performance

Our local theater group hosted an evening of storytelling featuring handcrafted sets, period costumes, and a cast of residents from across the county. Friends and families filled the seats to support their neighbors and enjoy a night of homegrown arts and culture.
