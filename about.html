About Macon County

Founded in 1828, Macon County, NC has grown into a strong and resilient mountain community guided by dedicated local leadership. Governed by a Board of Commissioners and supported by a range of county departments, Macon County works to provide essential services, promote public safety, and support the well-being of its residents. Through collaborative leadership and engaged citizens, the county continues to honor its heritage while supporting thoughtful, long-term growth.

Macon County takes pride in fostering a welcoming enviornment where residents, businesses, and visitors feel supported. County departments collaborate daily to streamline services, expand community programs, and enhance access to local resources. From maintaining vitual infrustructure to strengthening emergency readiness, Macon County remains committed to ensuring that every community member has the opportunity to thrive.
