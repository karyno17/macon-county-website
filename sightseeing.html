Sightseeing & Visitor Resources

Contact Header Section

From sweeping mountain views to hidden waterfalls and winding trails, Macon County offers unforgettable outdoor experiences at every turn. Visitors can enjoy serene parks, breathtaking scenic overlooks, and miles of well-loved hiking routes that folks have been following for generations.

Beyond the trails, our mountain towns - including Franklin, Highlands, Otto, and the Nantahala community - offer a vibrant mix of local businesses to explore. From charming boutiques and artisan shops to cozy cafes, wellness centers, and restaurants featuring regional flavors, each stop adds its own character to your visit. Complement your outdoor adventures with a stroll through these welcoming communities and experience the full beauty, flavor, and hospitality of Macon County.

Mountain Overlooks

Experience wide-open views across the Blue Ridge Mountains.

Family-Friendly Trails

Explore walking paths suitable for all ages and skill levels and through tranquil woodlands.

Outdoor Adventures

Enjoy camping, fishing, and scenic riverside spots. Fur friends are welcome on most trails!
